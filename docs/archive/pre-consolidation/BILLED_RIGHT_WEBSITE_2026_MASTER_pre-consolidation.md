# Billed Right Website 2026 --- Master Standard

> Governing strategy for the existing Billed Right website. Preserve the
> useful current design/code foundation; do not rebuild from scratch.

## North Star

**Market position:** Enterprise Revenue Performance Partner

**Approved core statement:**\
\> We help healthcare organizations achieve stronger financial
performance by transforming the way revenue operations are managed.

Billed Right must remain clearly recognizable as an experienced Revenue
Cycle Management company. Differentiation comes from combining RCM
expertise, scalable operations, data intelligence, automation,
predictive analytics, AI, and expert human oversight.

## Primary audiences

Prioritize enterprise physician groups, multi-location and
multi-specialty organizations, MSOs/healthcare platforms, private
equity-backed healthcare organizations, and established organizations
needing RCM performance improvement.

Address three situations: 1. **Growth & Scale** --- providers,
locations, specialties, acquisitions. 2. **Performance Improvement** ---
cash flow, collections, AR, denials, leakage, visibility, compliance,
productivity. 3. **Transformation** --- RCM modernization, integration,
consolidation, vendor/model replacement, automation/intelligence.

## Outcomes before services

Lead with stronger financial performance, predictable cash flow,
improved collections, faster reimbursement, reduced leakage, fewer
avoidable denials, stronger compliance/control, executive visibility,
better decisions, and scalability.

Medical billing, coding, credentialing, AR, eligibility, authorization
and payment posting remain capabilities and SEO topics, but do not
define the brand.

## Revenue Intelligence vision

Billed Right is evolving toward an integrated **Revenue Intelligence
Platform**.

`ANALYZE → PREDICT → PREVENT → AUTOMATE → ESCALATE → HUMAN JUDGMENT → LEARN → OPTIMIZE`

Automation/AI handle repeatable high-confidence work. Experienced RCM
professionals provide judgment, exception management, compliance
oversight, QA, strategic guidance and continuous improvement.

Every technology capability must be classified internally as: - LIVE /
PRODUCTION - LIMITED USE - PILOT - UNDER DEVELOPMENT - FUTURE VISION

Never present future capabilities as live.

## Homepage narrative

Use existing components where practical: 1. Hero / enterprise promise 2.
Enterprise credibility 3. Business challenges 4. Billed Right operating
model 5. Revenue Intelligence / automation / AI 6. Outcomes 7.
Capabilities 8. Who We Serve 9. Specialty/platform experience 10. Client
proof 11. Compliance/trust 12. Executive CTA

**Hero eyebrow:** Enterprise Revenue Performance Partner\
**H1:** Transform Revenue Operations. Strengthen Financial Performance.\
**Core supporting idea:** We help healthcare organizations achieve
stronger financial performance by transforming the way revenue
operations are managed.\
**Primary CTA:** Schedule an Executive Conversation\
**Secondary CTA:** Explore Our Approach

Do not lead with "Boost your practice revenue," "Let us handle the
billing --- you focus on care," or other small-practice/traditional
billing positioning.

## H1 and heading rules

Every important indexable page needs one clear primary H1, a unique
title, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, and search terminology naturally
represented in prominent headings. Never use heading tags merely for
styling.

Examples: - Enterprise Revenue Cycle Management Services - Denial
Management & Prevention Services - Medical Coding Services for
Healthcare Organizations - Healthcare Credentialing Services - Accounts
Receivable Management - \[Specialty\] Revenue Cycle Management

## Semantic keyword system

**Brand:** Enterprise Revenue Performance Partner; Revenue Performance;
stronger financial performance; Revenue Operations; Revenue
Intelligence; predictable cash flow; scalable revenue operations;
enterprise RCM; revenue transformation; executive visibility;
data-driven decision-making.

**Technology:** intelligent automation; predictive analytics; denial
prediction; denial prevention; AI-driven insights; workflow
intelligence; exception management; human oversight; data intelligence.

**SEO/service:** Revenue Cycle Management; medical billing services;
denial management; medical coding; credentialing services; accounts
receivable management; eligibility; prior authorization; payment
posting; specialty medical billing.

Use naturally. Never keyword-stuff.

## Internal linking

Build intentional paths:
`Executive Problem → Solution → Specialty → Case Study → Insight → CTA`

Pillar pages link to child pages; child pages link to pillars. Blogs
link contextually to commercial pages. Case studies link to relevant
solutions/specialties. Avoid orphan pages. Use descriptive anchors.

## Breadcrumbs

Use visible breadcrumbs on appropriate nested pages and matching
BreadcrumbList structured data, e.g.:
`Home → Solutions → Revenue Cycle Management → Denial Management`

## Footer site tree

Create a structured footer with columns: - Solutions - Who We Serve -
Technology & Revenue Intelligence - Specialties - Resources - Company

Include contact/trust information and primary CTA. Do not dump every URL
into the footer.

## XML sitemap, robots and canonicals

Maintain a valid XML sitemap containing canonical indexable pages.
Exclude redirects, duplicates, noindex pages, staging pages and
internal-search/utility pages not intended for search.

Maintain correct robots.txt. Do not accidentally block important render
resources. Production canonicals must never point to Netlify staging
URLs.

## llms.txt

Create `/llms.txt` as a concise curated machine-readable guide to
authoritative public content: - Company overview - Enterprise RCM -
Revenue Intelligence - Solutions - Who We Serve - Specialties -
Results/Case Studies - Leadership/About - Trust/Compliance -
Insights/Research

Do not treat llms.txt as a Google ranking factor or substitute for SEO.
Consider llms-full.txt only if it can be maintained accurately.

Do **not** create an "OKF" file unless a valid specification is provided
and reviewed.

## Structured data

Use only schema matching visible content. Consider Organization,
WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Person, Article/BlogPosting, and
VideoObject where appropriate. Never fabricate ratings/reviews or
misleading schema.

## 2026 blog/content standard

Do not mass-produce generic AI articles. Important content should add
first-hand RCM experience, original analysis, aggregated/anonymized
operational data, expert interpretation, case experience, a distinctive
Billed Right viewpoint, actionable frameworks, or verified current
context.

Each article should have: - clear search intent - one descriptive H1 -
logical H2/H3 - real author - reviewer where appropriate - published
date - substantive updated date when truly revised - evidence/sources
where appropriate - contextual internal links - contextual CTA -
accurate article schema - unique title/meta - useful answer/summary near
the top - no fabricated statistics/expertise

## Expert authorship

Prefer real named subject-matter experts for high-value content. Use
factual author bios and Person schema where appropriate. Do not
exaggerate credentials.

## Executive problem pages

Develop high-value pages where content quality supports them: - Improve
Healthcare Cash Flow - Reduce Revenue Leakage - Reduce Avoidable
Denials - Reduce Cost to Collect - Improve RCM Performance - Scale
Revenue Cycle Operations - Revenue Cycle Transformation - Revenue Cycle
Analytics - RCM Integration After Acquisition

## Resource center

Evolve Resources into an executive knowledge center: Insights, Case
Studies, Executive Guides, Calculators, Research/Benchmarks,

Performance benchmark research when data governance/sample quality
support it.

## Trust architecture

Create a dedicated trust/compliance area around verified HIPAA, security
controls, data governance, workforce training, business continuity, QA,
audit readiness, and responsible AI/AI governance as capabilities
mature.

### Mandatory SOC 2 rule

**Billed Right is NOT SOC 2 certified.**

Never state "SOC 2 Certified," use a SOC 2 certification badge, imply
certification, or use ambiguous wording reasonably interpreted as
certification. Any SOC 2-related wording requires explicit
Compliance/Legal approval and must accurately describe actual status.

Avoid absolute claims such as "100% secure."

## Claims validation

Never invent client outcomes, metrics, AI features, certifications,
awards, security claims, counts, revenue processed, integrations,
implementation timelines, guarantees or testimonials.

Use placeholders: - \[APPROVED METRIC NEEDED\] - \[COMPLIANCE REVIEW\] -
\[CONFIRM CURRENT CAPABILITY\] - \[VERIFIED CASE STUDY NEEDED\]

Maintain a claims register with claim, page/section, owner, evidence,
measurement period, approval status, publish wording and revalidation
date.

## CTA strategy

Every page needs a conversion path, not necessarily a large embedded
form.

Primary enterprise CTA: **Schedule an Executive Conversation**

Contextual CTAs: Request a Revenue Performance Assessment; Explore Our
Approach; View Client Results; Talk to an RCM Expert; Call Billed Right
where appropriate.

### Mobile

Design explicitly for mobile with a persistent but non-obstructive CTA
where appropriate, one-tap `tel:` calling, one-tap
consultation/assessment, short forms, thumb-friendly targets, and no
intrusive overlays.

## Forms

Forms must be mobile-friendly, accessible, low-friction, trackable and
clear about next steps. Enterprise forms may capture name, work email,
organization, role, scale, primary challenge, phone where appropriate,
and message.

## Metadata

Every indexable page: unique title, unique meta description, one primary
H1, canonical URL, social metadata where appropriate, descriptive alt
text and correct indexability. Avoid boilerplate titles.

## Technical quality

Prioritize Core Web Vitals, mobile performance, responsive layouts,
semantic HTML, keyboard usability, form labels, focus states, image
optimization, minimal unnecessary JS, stable layouts, correct status
codes, no broken links/redirect chains, HTTPS, canonicals and crawlable
HTML.

## Measurement

Track enterprise CTA clicks, form starts/completions, phone clicks,
mobile CTA use, assessment conversions, qualified lead rate,
landing-page conversion, case-study engagement, Revenue Intelligence
engagement, organic leads, search landing pages, referral/AI traffic
where identifiable, and enterprise vs small-practice lead mix.

Success is qualified enterprise opportunity generation, not traffic
alone.

## Search Console launch checklist

Verify production property; submit sitemap; check indexability/key URLs;
monitor Page Indexing/Core Web Vitals/security/manual
actions/structured-data errors; track query/page performance; identify
orphan/low-value pages; monitor redirects after migration.

## Preserve existing value

Do not rebuild functioning components for novelty. Preserve useful
visual system, typography, responsive framework, service/specialty
templates, case-study framework, resources/calculators, testimonials,
technology ecosystem, onboarding visuals, SEO inventory, lead capture
and tasteful motion.

## Final acceptance test

For every major page: 1. Purpose/search intent obvious? 2. One clear H1?
3. Correct hierarchy/breadcrumbs? 4. Strong internal links? 5.
Enterprise positioning reinforced where appropriate? 6. Original/useful
information? 7. Claims substantiated? 8. AI status accurate? 9. Clear
conversion path? 10. Excellent mobile experience? 11. Accurate
metadata/schema? 12. Fast, accessible, crawlable, indexable? 13.
Connected to relevant solution/proof/resources? 14. Would an enterprise
executive trust Billed Right?
