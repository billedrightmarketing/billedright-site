# 9. REASON #5 — INTELLIGENT RCM

## H2

## Technology With an Operating Purpose

Core model:

**Experienced RCM People + Practical AI & Automation + Data-to-Decisions + Disciplined Execution**

Visual:

**PREVENT → DETECT → PRIORITIZE → AUTOMATE → ACT → LEARN**

### Claim-Quality / Scrubbing Automation

**Technology:** evaluates defined claim information for exceptions.  
**Signal:** missing or inconsistent information.  
**Human action:** experienced staff review and resolve appropriate exceptions.  
**Problem addressed:** preventable rejection, denial and rework.

### Eligibility Automation

**Technology:** supports verification and exception identification.  
**Signal:** coverage issues.  
**Human action:** staff investigate.  
**Problem addressed:** invalid coverage entering the revenue cycle.

### Denial Classification / Pattern Detection

**Technology:** organizes denial information.  
**Signal:** recurring payer, provider, service or workflow patterns.  
**Human action:** teams resolve claims and address upstream causes.  
**Problem addressed:** repeated denials treated as isolated transactions.

### A/R Prioritization

**Technology:** prioritization / work-order logic.  
**Signal:** financially meaningful and actionable inventory.  
**Human action:** experienced teams work prioritized accounts.  
**Problem addressed:** recoverable revenue delayed behind lower-priority work.

### Analytics

**Technology:** organizes revenue-cycle signals.  
**Signal:** changes in performance.  
**Human action:** leadership and Billed Right identify priorities.  
**Problem addressed:** reports without action.

Required callout:

> **AI does not replace experienced revenue-cycle professionals. It helps them see more, prioritize better and act earlier.**

Do not use generic phrases such as “revolutionary AI” or “fully autonomous RCM.”

Do not present roadmap capabilities as live.
