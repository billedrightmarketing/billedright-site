## 7. Billed Right GI Revenue-Cycle Model

### One Accountable Revenue Cycle Across GI Services

Use:

**VERIFY → CAPTURE → SUBMIT → MONITOR → PRIORITIZE → RESOLVE → POST → ANALYZE → EDUCATE → IMPROVE**

Explain each through financial execution, not generic definitions.

- **Verify:** eligibility/coverage information.
- **Capture:** required billing information supported by available documentation.
- **Submit:** professional claim workflows appropriate to service, setting and payer.
- **Monitor:** rejections, claim status, denials, payments and exceptions.
- **Prioritize:** direct experienced staff toward financially important/actionable work.
- **Resolve:** rejected, denied and unpaid claims.
- **Post:** payment posting/reconciliation within scope.
- **Analyze:** payer, denial, A/R and service-line trends.
- **Educate:** connect recurring coding/documentation patterns to appropriate education/corrective workflow.
- **Improve:** use patterns to reduce preventable rework.

Do not imply Billed Right determines clinical diagnoses or that Medical Coding is automatically included.
