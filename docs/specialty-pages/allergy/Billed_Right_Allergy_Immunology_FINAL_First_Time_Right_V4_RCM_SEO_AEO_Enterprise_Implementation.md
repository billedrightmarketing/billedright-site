# 7. BILLED RIGHT'S ALLERGY RCM MODEL

## H2

## Disciplined Revenue-Cycle Execution From Eligibility Through A/R

Use:

**VERIFY → CAPTURE → SCRUB → SUBMIT → MONITOR → PRIORITIZE → RESOLVE → POST → ANALYZE → IMPROVE**

### VERIFY

Support eligibility and coverage workflows.

### CAPTURE

Ensure required billing information enters the revenue-cycle workflow.

### SCRUB

Identify defined claim exceptions before submission.

### SUBMIT

Execute claim submission within contracted scope.

### MONITOR

Track rejections, denials, unpaid claims and payment status.

### PRIORITIZE

Focus revenue-cycle teams on financially meaningful and actionable work.

### RESOLVE

Work rejected, denied and unpaid claims.

### POST

Maintain payment-posting/reconciliation workflows within contracted scope.

### ANALYZE

Identify payer, service, provider and A/R patterns.

### IMPROVE

Use recurring findings to strengthen future execution and provider education.
