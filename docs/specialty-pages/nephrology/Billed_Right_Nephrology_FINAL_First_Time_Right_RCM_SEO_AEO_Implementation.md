# 3. EXTERNAL SPECIALTY FACTS THAT INFORM THE PAGE

Use these concepts to demonstrate specialty understanding without turning the page into a regulatory tutorial.

## ESRD Monthly Capitation Payment

CMS describes MCP as payment to physicians for most dialysis-related physician services furnished to Medicare ESRD patients on a monthly basis.

The number and type of physician visits can affect appropriate MCP billing. This makes dialysis-related professional revenue workflows materially different from ordinary office-visit billing.

**Marketing implication:** Billed Right should demonstrate understanding of dialysis/MCP workflow complexity, documentation, claim accuracy, recurring revenue and denial risk.

Do not create a large CPT-code table.

## ESRD Medicare Secondary Payer Coordination

CMS states that when an individual has ESRD, has applicable group health plan coverage, and is within the ESRD 30-month coordination period, the group health plan generally pays primary and Medicare pays secondary.

**Marketing implication:** Nephrology organizations can face payer-order and coordination complexity that requires accurate eligibility, insurance information, payer sequencing and follow-up.

Do not turn the page into an MSP compliance manual.

## ESRD Facility PPS vs. Physician RCM

CMS pays ESRD facilities under an ESRD Prospective Payment System on a per-treatment basis, with consolidated-billing rules applying to specified renal dialysis services.

This is distinct from physician MCP/professional billing.

**Important implementation rule:** Do not blur:

**physician/professional Nephrology RCM**

with

**ESRD facility PPS/consolidated billing**

unless Billed Right's actual contracted scope for a client includes the relevant facility work.

The page may demonstrate understanding of the ecosystem, but it must not claim Billed Right operates a service that has not been internally verified.

## Practical Nephrology Examples

### Claim-Quality Automation

**Technology:** checks claims/workflows for defined issues and exceptions before or during submission.

**Signal:** missing, inconsistent or potentially problematic claim information.

**Human action:** experienced RCM staff review the exception and take the appropriate billing action.

**Problem addressed:** preventable rejection, rework or delayed payment.

### Eligibility & Coverage Automation

**Technology:** supports eligibility verification and exception identification.

**Signal:** coverage changes, verification issues or payer information requiring attention.

**Human action:** staff investigate and resolve the coverage issue according to the workflow.

**Problem addressed:** claims entering the wrong payer path or being delayed because of coverage problems.

### Denial Classification & Pattern Detection

**Technology:** groups and analyzes denials to surface recurring causes, payer patterns, service-line patterns or operational trends.

**Signal:** repeated denial categories or concentrations.

**Human action:** Nephrology RCM teams resolve claims and escalate recurring root causes for corrective action.

**Problem addressed:** repeated denials being treated as isolated transactions.

### A/R Work Prioritization

**Technology:** uses work-order/prioritization logic to organize follow-up.

**Signal:** accounts or claims requiring higher-priority attention based on defined factors.

**Human action:** RCM teams work the prioritized inventory and document resolution.

**Problem addressed:** high-value or actionable revenue waiting behind lower-priority work.

### Authorization Exception Visibility — Only When Contracted

**Technology:** tracking/exception workflows can help surface authorization issues where Prior Authorization is part of the client's separately contracted scope.

**Signal:** missing, expiring or unresolved authorization-related exceptions.

**Human action:** the appropriate team investigates and acts according to the contracted workflow.

**Problem addressed:** downstream authorization-related revenue risk.

Do not imply Prior Authorization is standard RCM.

### Data-to-Decisions

**Technology:** consolidates operational and financial signals into usable reporting.

**Signal:** payer, denial, A/R, service-line or workflow performance changes.

**Human action:** Billed Right and client leadership use the information to identify priorities and actions.

**Problem addressed:** executives having data but not knowing where financial performance is breaking down.
