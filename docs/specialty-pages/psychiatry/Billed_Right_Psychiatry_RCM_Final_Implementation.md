## 5. WHY PSYCHIATRY RCM IS DIFFERENT — NEW / REWRITE

### H2
## Psychiatry Billing Is Different Because Psychiatric Care Is Different

Psychiatry combines clinical services, time-based treatment, medication management, psychotherapy, telehealth and multiple provider types within a payer environment that can vary significantly by plan and service.

For growing Psychiatry organizations, small breakdowns in documentation, eligibility, claim submission, payer requirements or follow-up can compound across hundreds or thousands of encounters.

### Time-Based Services
Psychotherapy services often depend on accurate documentation of time and service performed. Inconsistent documentation can create claim delays, reduced reimbursement or denials.

### E/M + Psychotherapy
Medication management and psychotherapy may occur during the same encounter. The revenue cycle must accurately reflect the services documented and applicable payer requirements.

### Telepsychiatry
Telehealth remains central to many Psychiatry organizations, while payer rules, modifiers, place-of-service requirements and coverage policies can vary.

### Payer & Coverage Complexity
Mental-health benefits, eligibility, authorization requirements and payer-specific reimbursement policies can create revenue leakage when issues are not identified and managed early.

### Provider & Organizational Complexity
Psychiatrists, advanced practice providers and other behavioral-health professionals may operate across multiple locations, payer contracts and care models—making consistency increasingly important as the organization grows.

## 6. TOP REVENUE LEAKS — REWRITE

### H2
## Where Psychiatry Organizations Commonly Lose Revenue

Present approximately six highly scannable revenue leaks.

### Documentation & Service Mismatch
When the billed service is not fully supported by documentation, claims may be delayed, reduced or denied.

**Impact:** avoidable rework, delayed reimbursement and lost revenue.

### E/M and Psychotherapy Complexity
Medication-management and psychotherapy encounters create billing complexity when documentation, service selection and payer requirements are not aligned.

**Impact:** rejected/denied claims and inconsistent reimbursement.

### Telepsychiatry Billing Variability
Payer differences can create errors involving coverage, modifiers or place of service.

**Impact:** preventable denials and delayed cash.

### Eligibility & Behavioral-Health Benefit Issues
Behavioral-health benefits may differ from broader medical benefits.

**Impact:** unexpected non-covered services, patient balance issues and additional staff work.

### Authorization-Related Revenue Risk
Some services or payer arrangements may require authorization or have visit limitations.

**Impact:** reimbursement risk when payer requirements are missed.

**Important:** This describes a revenue risk. Do not imply Prior Authorization is included in core RCM.

### Denials & Aging A/R
Unresolved payer issues and inconsistent follow-up can push balances deeper into A/R.

**Impact:** slower cash flow, higher collection cost and reduced visibility.

## 18. FAQs — REWRITE FOR BUYER INTENT

### H2
## Psychiatry Revenue Cycle Management FAQs

### What makes Psychiatry revenue cycle management different from general medical billing?
Psychiatry combines time-based services, medication management, psychotherapy, telehealth and payer-specific behavioral-health requirements. These factors can create documentation, reimbursement and denial patterns that differ from many other specialties.

### How does Billed Right help reduce Psychiatry denials?
Billed Right analyzes rejection and denial patterns, works unresolved claims, identifies recurring root causes and uses operational feedback to help reduce preventable revenue loss over time.

### Can Billed Right support E/M visits performed with psychotherapy?
Yes. Billed Right understands the revenue-cycle complexity created when medication management and psychotherapy occur during the same encounter. The exact service scope—including whether Medical Coding is included—depends on the engagement.

### Does Billed Right support telepsychiatry billing?
Billed Right supports revenue-cycle workflows for Psychiatry organizations providing telehealth services and can help identify recurring payer, claim and reimbursement issues related to telepsychiatry.

### Does Billed Right provide Medical Coding for Psychiatry?
Medical Coding can be available as an additional service depending on the organization's needs. It should not be assumed to be included automatically in every core RCM engagement.

### Does Billed Right provide Prior Authorization services?
Prior Authorization support may be available as an additional service depending on scope. Billed Right also tracks authorization-related denial patterns as part of revenue-cycle performance analysis.

### Which Psychiatry EMRs does Billed Right support?
Billed Right can work within virtually any EMR or practice-management environment. Our experience includes eClinicalWorks, athenahealth, AdvancedMD, IMS, CentralReach and CareCloud.

### Can Billed Right support a multi-location Psychiatry group?
Yes. Billed Right's revenue-cycle model is designed to support growing organizations that need consistent workflows, payer follow-up, analytics and visibility across multiple providers and locations.

### What should a growing Psychiatry group look for in an RCM partner?
Look beyond claim submission. A strong RCM partner should understand Psychiatry-specific reimbursement issues, identify preventable revenue leakage, provide clear performance data, support your technology environment and have the operational capacity to scale with the organization.

### How do we know if our Psychiatry revenue cycle is performing well?
Leadership should evaluate more than total collections. Important indicators can include A/R aging, denial and rejection trends, payer performance, collection performance, revenue leakage and how quickly recurring issues are identified and corrected.
