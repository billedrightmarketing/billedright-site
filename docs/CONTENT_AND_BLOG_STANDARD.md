# Content and Blog Standard

## Principle
Do not mass-produce generic AI content.

High-value content should add first-hand RCM experience, original analysis, aggregated/anonymized data, expert interpretation, case experience, Billed Right point of view, actionable frameworks, or verified current context.

## Existing Blog Inventory
Classify every article:
- KEEP
- UPDATE
- CONSOLIDATE
- REDIRECT
- NOINDEX
- REMOVE

## Article Requirements
- clear search intent
- one descriptive H1
- logical H2/H3
- real author
- reviewer where appropriate
- published date
- meaningful updated date only after substantive revision
- evidence/sources where relevant
- contextual internal links
- contextual CTA
- accurate Article/BlogPosting schema
- unique title/meta
- useful answer/summary near top
- no fabricated statistics/expertise

## Priority Themes
- denial prediction/prevention
- revenue leakage
- cash-flow predictability
- cost to collect
- RCM transformation
- acquisition/integration
- scaling 25–100 provider organizations
- executive RCM metrics
- predictive analytics
- intelligent Revenue Operations
- AI + human oversight
- specialty-specific revenue performance
- Florida/Central Florida RCM insights where valuable

## Specialty Authority
Prioritize Cardiology, Pain Management, Internal Medicine, Behavioral Health.

Cluster model:
`Specialty Page → Challenges → Solutions → Insights → Case Studies → Technology → CTA`

## Platform Authority
Where verified: eClinicalWorks, IMS, AdvancedMD, Athena.
Only create meaningful pages with real expertise.

## Expert Authorship
Prefer named SMEs. Do not exaggerate credentials.

## Case Study Standard
1. Organization context
2. Baseline
3. Business problem
4. Diagnosis
5. Intervention
6. Process/technology
7. Results
8. Measurement period
9. Operational change
10. Executive/client perspective
11. Related links
12. CTA

Only verified facts.

## Resource Center
Insights, Case Studies, Executive Guides, Calculators, Research/Benchmarks, Webinars/Video, FAQs.
