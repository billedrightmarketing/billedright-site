# 2. MARKET-STUDY INSIGHT

Healthcare financial management connects accounting, budgeting, revenue cycle, cash flow and financial analysis.

Current MGMA financial-management guidance treats revenue cycle management, cash flow, budgeting, financial analysis, collections and A/R as interconnected parts of medical-practice financial management.

The 2026 MGMA Financials and Operations Data Report specifically emphasizes questions such as:

- where cash is slowing down;
- whether revenue growth is creating enough financial capacity;
- whether A/R, payer delays or patient balances are affecting cash conversion;
- how practices should use revenue and operating data for budgeting, staffing and reinvestment decisions.

This is exactly the gap the CPA page should address.

## What the CPA Sees

The CPA may see:

- cash below expectations;
- growing A/R;
- collections lagging production;
- margin pressure;
- financial results that do not match perceived patient volume;
- inconsistent deposits;
- expansion that is not generating expected cash;
- large adjustments;
- unexplained payer/payment patterns.

## What Billed Right Can Reveal

The revenue-cycle operation may reveal:

- eligibility issues;
- claim-quality problems;
- payer denials;
- delayed follow-up;
- high-value A/R;
- provider/location variation;
- reimbursement timing;
- workflow gaps;
- reconciliation issues;
- payer-specific performance;
- operational leakage.

The page should make that connection explicit.

# 14. CPA HEALTHCARE REVENUE-CYCLE REVIEW

## H2

## Questions to Ask When Reviewing a Healthcare Client's Revenue Cycle

This should be a high-value on-page section and can later become a downloadable resource.

### Cash Conversion

- Is collected revenue keeping pace with production?
- Is cash conversion changing even though patient volume is stable or increasing?
- Are payer delays affecting operating cash?

### A/R

- Is total A/R growing faster than revenue?
- How much A/R is aging beyond expected payer timelines?
- Which payers, providers or locations drive the aging?
- Is management separating actionable A/R from balances requiring different intervention?

### Denials

- Are denials increasing?
- What are the largest recurring denial categories?
- Are teams correcting root causes or only reworking individual claims?

### Growth

- Are new providers generating expected collections?
- Are new locations converting patient volume into cash?
- Is revenue-cycle staffing/process scaling appropriately?

### Reporting

- Can management explain what changed in collections and A/R?
- Are revenue-cycle reports decision-ready or merely transactional?
- Can the practice connect operational RCM changes to broader financial performance?

### Accountability

- Who owns the corrective action?
- What will be different next month or next quarter?
- Is progress measurable?
