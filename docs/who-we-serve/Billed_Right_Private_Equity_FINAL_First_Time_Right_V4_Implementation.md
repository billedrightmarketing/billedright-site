# 28. FAQ SECTION

## Private Equity Revenue-Cycle FAQs

### Why does revenue cycle matter to a healthcare investment?

Revenue-cycle performance affects cash conversion, A/R, collections, operating efficiency and management visibility. In physician-practice and healthcare-services platforms, those factors can influence working capital and operating performance.

### Can Billed Right support pre-close diligence?

Billed Right can support revenue-cycle diligence and operational assessment, including A/R, denials, workflows, reporting and integration readiness. Billed Right is not a Quality of Earnings, valuation, legal or tax-diligence provider.

### Can Billed Right help integrate add-on acquisitions?

Yes. Billed Right can help assess the acquired revenue-cycle operation, protect continuity, standardize agreed workflows and reporting, and integrate the asset into the platform's operating model.

### Does every practice need to use the same EMR?

No. Common operating standards and KPI definitions can be established even when a healthcare platform operates multiple EMR and practice-management systems.

### Can Billed Right manage RCM across an entire platform?

Yes, depending on engagement scope. Billed Right can support asset-level or broader portfolio/platform RCM models.

### Can Billed Right support only one portfolio company?

Yes. Asset-level engagements can be scoped independently.

### How does Billed Right use AI and automation?

Billed Right uses practical automation and AI-assisted workflows to help identify claim exceptions, denial patterns, A/R priorities and other revenue-cycle signals. Experienced RCM professionals determine and execute the appropriate action.

### Does Billed Right provide Prior Authorization, Credentialing or Medical Coding?

These and other Holistic Services may be available as separately scoped services depending on the engagement.

### Can Billed Right help prepare for exit?

Billed Right can help improve revenue-cycle reporting clarity, A/R visibility and documentation of operating performance within its scope. Billed Right does not provide valuation, investment-banking or sell-side Quality of Earnings services.
