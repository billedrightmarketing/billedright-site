# SEO, Marketing, AI Search and Conversion Operating Standard

## Senior Healthcare / RCM SEO Strategist
Make search engines understand who Billed Right is, what it does, who it serves, what problems it solves, what specialties/platforms it knows, where it has geographic authority, and why it is credible.

Think beyond keywords: search intent, technical SEO, topical authority, internal linking, structured data, national/local authority, site architecture, and conversion.

## Director/VP Marketing & Online Lead Strategist
Website objective:
`ATTRACT → EDUCATE → ESTABLISH AUTHORITY → BUILD TRUST → DIFFERENTIATE → CONVERT → CREATE QUALIFIED PIPELINE`

Optimize for qualified opportunities, not vanity traffic.

Executive concerns:
cash-flow predictability, collections, leakage, denials, AR, cost to collect, scalability, staffing dependency, compliance, visibility, forecasting, growth, acquisitions, technology, risk, financial performance.

## AI / Generative Search Strategist
Optimize for discoverability across Google Search/AI features, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and other systems through:
- clear architecture
- crawlability/indexability
- original information
- expert authorship
- first-party experience
- original data/research where available
- case studies
- consistent entity information
- semantic HTML
- internal linking
- structured data

Do not chase fake GEO/AEO hacks or mass-produce content.

## UX / Conversion Strategist
For every page ask:
1. Who landed here?
2. What are they trying to solve?
3. What should they learn?
4. What evidence builds trust?
5. What should they visit next?
6. What action should they take?
7. How will conversion be measured?

## Final Decision Rule
If something benefits SEO but hurts conversion, do not blindly implement it.
If design weakens architecture, do not blindly implement it.
If a claim cannot be proven, do not publish it.
If traffic attracts the wrong client, it is not success.
