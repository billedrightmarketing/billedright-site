# Billed Right Website 2026 Master Strategy

## Objective
Build a website that attracts the right healthcare organizations, establishes Billed Right as a sophisticated Revenue Cycle Management partner, performs strongly in traditional and AI-assisted search, and converts qualified visitors into conversations.

Preserve useful existing design/code. Correct positioning and architecture rather than rebuilding for novelty.

## Market Position
**Enterprise Revenue Performance Partner**

> We help healthcare organizations achieve stronger financial performance by transforming the way revenue operations are managed.

Revenue Cycle Management must remain clear for buyer understanding and SEO.

## Primary Audiences
CEOs, CFOs, COOs, Revenue Cycle leaders, practice executives, MSO/platform leaders, PE operating executives.

## Three Buying Situations
### Growth & Scale
Providers, locations, specialties, acquisitions.

### Performance Improvement
Cash flow, collections, AR, denials, leakage, visibility, compliance, productivity.

### Transformation
Modernizing RCM, integrating acquisitions, consolidating workflows, changing vendors/models, introducing automation/intelligence.

## Homepage Narrative
1. Hero / enterprise promise
2. Enterprise credibility
3. Business challenges
4. Billed Right operating model
5. Revenue Intelligence / automation / AI
6. Outcomes
7. Capabilities
8. Who We Serve
9. Specialty/platform experience
10. Client proof
11. Compliance/trust
12. Executive CTA

Hero:
- Eyebrow: `Enterprise Revenue Performance Partner`
- H1: `Transform Revenue Operations. Strengthen Financial Performance.`
- Core statement: `We help healthcare organizations achieve stronger financial performance by transforming the way revenue operations are managed.`
- Primary CTA: `Schedule an Executive Conversation`
- Secondary CTA: `Explore Our Approach`

Do not lead with small-practice/traditional billing language.

## Site Architecture
### Solutions
- Revenue Cycle Management
- Denial Management & Prevention
- Accounts Receivable Management
- Medical Coding
- Credentialing
- Eligibility & Prior Authorization
- Revenue Analytics / Reporting

### Who We Serve
- Physician Groups
- Multi-Location & Multi-Specialty Organizations
- MSOs & Healthcare Platforms
- Private Equity-Backed Healthcare
- Growing Healthcare Organizations

### Technology & Revenue Intelligence
- Revenue Intelligence
- Intelligent Automation
- Predictive Analytics
- AI in Revenue Cycle
- Integrations
- Reporting & Executive Intelligence

### Specialties
Prioritize Cardiology, Pain Management, Internal Medicine, Behavioral Health.

### Client Results
Case Studies, Client Stories, verified outcomes.

### Resources
Insights, Executive Guides, Calculators, Research/Benchmarks, Webinars/Video, FAQs.

### Company
About, Leadership, Trust/Compliance, Careers, Contact.

## EHR / Platform Authority
Where verified, build meaningful authority around eClinicalWorks, IMS, AdvancedMD, Athena.
Do not create thin platform pages solely for keywords.

## Florida Authority
Build legitimate authority around Florida and Central Florida without limiting national positioning.
Use local/company information, Florida-specific insights, approved local case studies, relevant schema/location signals, internal linking, and useful Florida landing content.
Avoid thin city pages.

## Conversion
Every page needs a conversion path.
Primary: `Schedule an Executive Conversation`
Contextual: `Request a Revenue Performance Assessment`, `Talk to an RCM Expert`, `View Client Results`, `Call Billed Right`.

Mobile: one-tap call, one-tap consultation/assessment, short forms, persistent but non-obstructive CTA where appropriate.

## Success
Track qualified 25–100 provider / $25M–$250M opportunities, priority specialty leads, Florida/Central Florida opportunities, organic qualified leads, AI/referral opportunities, assessments, calls, and pipeline generated.

### Enterprise RCM Pillar
Create a materially deep enterprise destination covering multi-location/multi-specialty complexity, growth, acquisitions, transition, governance/SLAs, reporting, compliance, integrations, scalability, executive engagement, and verified outcomes. Do not clone generic RCM copy.

### Revenue Intelligence Authority Page
Explain what Revenue Intelligence means at Billed Right: business outcomes, current capabilities, human oversight, predictive model, automation philosophy, executive intelligence, and future platform vision. Separate live capability from future direction using the LIVE/LIMITED/PILOT/UNDER DEVELOPMENT/FUTURE VISION status system.

### Trust Architecture
Create a dedicated trust/compliance area around verified HIPAA practices, security controls, data governance, workforce training, business continuity, QA, audit readiness, and responsible AI/AI governance as capabilities mature. Every claim in this area is gated through `docs/claims-register.md` — see the mandatory SOC 2 rule in `docs/BILLED_RIGHT_FACTS_AND_GUARDRAILS.md`.

### Enterprise Form Fields
Enterprise-facing forms may capture: name, work email, organization, role, organization scale (providers/locations/revenue band), primary challenge, phone (where appropriate), and message. Keep forms short and mobile-friendly.
