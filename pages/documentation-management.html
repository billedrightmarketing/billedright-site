Medical Practice Documentation Management Services

Documentation management is the process of routing incoming faxes and documents to the correct patient charts and staff members within the practice management system. An unmanaged document inbox creates clinical risk — lab results, referral letters, and authorization responses sit unprocessed while no one realizes anything is missing until it's needed. Billed Right checks faxes daily, assigns each document to the correct chart with a clear status and urgency level, and completes all daily faxes within 12 to 24 hours of receipt.

What an Unmanaged Fax Inbox Costs the Practice

Incoming fax volume in a busy medical practice is consistently underestimated, because most of it arrives quietly in the background while everyone's attention is on the schedule in front of them. Lab results, referral letters, prior authorization responses, patient records, and insurance correspondence all come through the same fax inbox, and each one has to be routed to the right person or chart before it does any good. A lab result that never reaches the ordering physician isn't just a filing problem — it's a result no one acted on.

Without a dedicated process, that routing work falls to whoever has a spare minute, which in practice means it happens inconsistently. Documents pile up between other priorities, get assigned to the wrong chart in the rush to clear the queue, or sit with no indication of how urgent they are next to routine correspondence that could wait. The clinical risk is real, but so is the billing risk — an authorization response that never reaches billing looks, from the claim's perspective, exactly like an authorization that was never obtained.

44%
Studies in clinical operations management show that unprocessed or misrouted medical documents are a contributing factor in up to 44% of preventable adverse clinical events — making document routing a patient safety issue, not just an administrative one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of documents does Billed Right manage in the fax inbox?

Lab results, referral letters, prior authorization responses, patient records, and insurance correspondence are among the document types most commonly routed through the fax inbox, and each is handled according to its own urgency and destination.

How quickly does Billed Right process incoming faxes?

The fax inbox is checked daily, and all daily faxes are completed within 12 to 24 hours of receipt, so documents don't sit unprocessed waiting for someone to notice them.

How does Billed Right decide which staff member to route a document to?

Routing decisions are based on document type and coordinated closely with local staff, so a lab result goes to the clinical team, an authorization response goes to billing, and the practice's own workflow preferences are respected rather than a generic routing rule applied across every practice.

Does Billed Right work within our existing practice management system?

Yes. Faxes are checked and routed directly within the fax module of the practice's existing practice management system, so there's no separate document platform to log into or reconcile.

How does Billed Right prioritize urgent documents over routine correspondence?

Every document is assigned a status and urgency level when it's routed, so a critical lab result is flagged for immediate attention instead of sitting in the same queue as routine correspondence.

What reporting does Billed Right provide on daily document management activity?

A daily report shows faxes received versus completed, so the practice has full visibility into document volume and processing status rather than having to trust that the inbox is being kept current.
