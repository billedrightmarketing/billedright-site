We are thrilled to announce that **Billed Right has achieved the ISO 27001:2022 certification**, a globally recognized standard for Information Security Management Systems. This achievement represents our unwavering commitment to safeguarding sensitive data and upholding the trust of our clients and partners in the Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) sector.

[This milestone is a testament to the collective efforts of our team across all locations—[Chennai, Trichy, and Longwood](https://billedright.com/locations/)—who worked tirelessly to align our operations with the stringent requirements of ISO 27001:2022.

This achievement builds upon our existing **QMS ISO 9001:2015 certification**, which underscores our dedication to quality management and operational excellence. Together, these certifications highlight our dual commitment to delivering the highest standards of both quality and security, setting us apart as a trusted partner in the RCM space.

### **Why This Matters**

In an industry where security and confidentiality are paramount, the ISO 27001:2022 certification serves as an assurance that Billed Right has implemented best practices to protect your data. This achievement underscores our mission to not only meet industry standards but to exceed expectations by delivering secure, efficient, and reliable solutions for your RCM needs.

### **Key Highlights of This Achievement**

- **Enhanced Data Protection:** Your sensitive information is now secured under one of the most stringent global standards for data security.
- **Operational Excellence:** Certified processes that ensure efficiency and reliability in every aspect of our service.
- **Commitment to Trust:** A reinforced pledge to be your trusted advisor, safeguarding your success.

### **A Team Effort Across Borders**

[This milestone would not have been possible without the collaboration, dedication, and hard work of our teams in [Chennai, Trichy, and Longwood](https://billedright.com/locations/). Their combined efforts, along with the unwavering support of our clients and partners, played a critical role in achieving this recognition.
