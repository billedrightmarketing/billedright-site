From July 31 to August 3, the Caribe Royale Resort in Orlando was filled with energy, ideas, and inspiration as we joined physicians, practice leaders, and healthcare innovators from across the country for the **42nd Annual AOA Convention**. As a proud sponsor, we had the privilege of not just attending—but truly connecting.

Our team, **Maryan Hennen, MBA** and **Jeffrey Shaw**, spent the weekend in conversations that mattered, discussing the everyday challenges practices face, sharing ways to **maximize reimbursements**, **reduce AR days**, and **unlock hidden revenue**.

One of our biggest takeaways? Healthcare leaders are looking for **tailored, transparent solutions **and that’s exactly where we thrive. Listening to practitioners share their stories reminded us why our work matters: every dollar recovered and every hour saved translates directly into better patient care.

We’re grateful for everyone who stopped by our booth, shared their experiences, and explored how partnering with Billed Right could help them turn **numbers into growth**.
