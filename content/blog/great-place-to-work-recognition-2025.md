At Billed Right, we know that our success begins with our people. That’s why we’re incredibly proud to share that we’ve been officially **Great Place to Work® Certified for the second consecutive year**!

This recognition is more than just an achievement—it’s a reflection of the **culture we’ve built together**. Being certified two years in a row highlights the trust, respect, and sense of purpose that define life at Billed Right. It’s proof that when we prioritize our employees, we create a workplace where innovation thrives, collaboration is natural, and everyone feels valued.

Over the past year, we’ve continued to focus on what matters most: **supporting our team members’ growth, encouraging open communication, and fostering an inclusive environment**. From professional development opportunities to celebrating big wins as a team, we remain committed to creating a space where each person can reach their full potential.

We owe this recognition to our incredible employees—the heart of Billed Right. Your dedication, passion, and hard work make our company what it is today, and this certification belongs to you.

As we look ahead to 2025 and beyond, we’re excited to build on this momentum. Together, we’ll continue to strengthen our culture, push boundaries, and ensure Billed Right remains not just a workplace, but a community where people thrive.

Here’s to many more years of being a **Great Place to Work®**—because when our people succeed, we all succeed.
