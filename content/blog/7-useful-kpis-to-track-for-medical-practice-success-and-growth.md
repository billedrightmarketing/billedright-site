### **Patient Experience:**

Focusing on Net Promoter Score and positive online reviews ensures a patient-centric approach, fostering satisfaction and loyalty.

### **KPI #4 Net Promoter Score (NPS)**

**Explanation: **NPS gauges patient satisfaction by asking them how likely they are to recommend your practice, providing a valuable indicator of overall patient experience.

**How to Calculate: **Net Promoter Score is calculated based on a single question survey: “On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend our practice to a friend or colleague?”. The respondents of the survey are then categorized into one of three groups.

- Promoters (score 9-10): These are loyal patients who are most likely to recommend your practice to the individuals around them.
- Passives: (score 7-8): These patients are satisfied but are not too enthusiastic and could be easily swayed by other providers.
- Detractors: (score 0-6): These are dissatisfied patients who may harm your practice’s reputation

### **Operational Efficiency:**

Monitoring time spent on administrative tasks and claims processing accuracy reflects operational efficiency, and optimizing resource allocation.

### **KPI #6 Time spent on administrative tasks per patient visit**

**Explanation: **This KPI evaluates the efficiency of administrative processes by measuring the time staff spend on non-clinical tasks per patient visit.

**How to Calculate: **

- **Define Administrative Tasks:** Clearly outline which tasks are considered administrative. These could include paperwork, data entry, scheduling, etc.
- **Measure Time Spent:** Track the time spent on administrative tasks during a specific period (e.g., a day, week, or month).
- **Count Patient Visits:** Determine the total number of patient visits during the same period.

​\( \textbf{Time Spent on Admin Tasks Per Patient} = \left ( \textbf{Total Time Spent on Admin Tasks} \over \textbf{Number of Patient Visists}\displaystyle \right ) \)

**The result will give you the average time spent on administrative tasks per patient visit.**
