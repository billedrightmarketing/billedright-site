As we progress in the use of telemedicine to assist patients in minimizing the risk of contagion, one key concern has already been addressed, several states have loosened up most licensing requirements to facilitate emergency medical care beyond state lines. Not all states, however, have waived these restrictions, and physician compliance is expected.
